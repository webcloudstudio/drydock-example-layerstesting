Acceptance scope

Sea Trials assess ledger workflows and their observable results. Database preparation is a prerequisite exercised by the supplied grader, not a separate release criterion. Verification uses the declared ledger commands and supplied data; it does not introduce schema mutations, schema-replacement requirements, or database-engine qualification tests. Stack and persistence implementation constraints remain in the build instructions.

The supplied grader and the behavioral criteria below are required release evidence. Its exit status establishes grader conformance; it is not the sole verdict for all Sea Trials. Criterion identifiers are retained; st-002, st-003, and st-009 are retired.

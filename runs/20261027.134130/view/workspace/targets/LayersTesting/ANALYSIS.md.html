Planning Instructions

Delivery Shape

The system is a single-executable CLI named ledger at the application root. Inputs are CSV files and command-line arguments. Outputs are CSV to standard output on success and one diagnostic line to standard error on failure. Persistence is SQLite through the shipped persistence library, with three tables: accounts, categories, and transactions. Amounts are stored as integer cents.

Required execution flow: every command validates all inputs and business rules (including the non-negative balance rule) before its first write, and every write either completes in full or changes nothing.

Test and Acceptance Strategy

Story-scoped tests: each story runs the grader slice it owns, or the application's bin/test.sh scoped to the relevant cases. No story runs the full grader.

Terminal verification story: the grader sh sources/run.sh runs unfiltered and must exit 0. That is the single full-suite gate, and it depends on every implementation story. Its acceptance assertion declares Suite: full.

Final Sea Trial verification is recorded in SEA_TRIALS.md.

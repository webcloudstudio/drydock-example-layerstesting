<requirement name="atomic-commands" stories="db-transaction,load-accounts,load-categories,load-transactions,transfer">
**Every command is one database transaction.** A command that fails for any reason leaves every table exactly as it was. A load that rejects row 11 stores none of rows 1 to 10. A transfer stores both of its rows or neither.
</requirement>
<requirement name="no-negative-balance" stories="db-balance,load-transactions">
No command leaves an account balance below zero. An account's balance is the sum of its transaction amounts. Posting, amending, deleting, transferring, and loading are all subject to this rule, checked after the command's changes and before it commits.
</requirement>
<requirement name="account-balance" stories="account-get,report-balances">
An account's balance is the sum of its transaction amounts.
</requirement>
<requirement name="code-format" stories="val-codes">
**Codes** (`account`, `category`) are 1 to 12 characters from `A-Z` and `0-9`.
</requirement>
<requirement name="text-length" stories="val-codes">
**Names and descriptions** are 1 to 40 characters. **Memos** are 0 to 60 characters. None of them contains a comma or a newline.
</requirement>
<requirement name="amount-format" stories="val-amount">
**Amounts** on input match `-?DIGITS[.D[D]]`: at most two decimal places, never zero. On output an amount is written with exactly two decimal places, a leading `-` when negative, and no thousands separator: `-12.50`, `0.00`, `5000.00`.
</requirement>
<requirement name="date-format" stories="val-dates">
**Dates** are real calendar dates written `YYYY-MM-DD`. `2026-02-30` is rejected.
</requirement>
<requirement name="assign-next-transaction-id" stories="txn-post,transfer">
A posted transaction receives one more than the largest `txn_id` present, or `1` in an empty table. A transfer's outgoing row takes that id and its incoming row the next.
</requirement>
<requirement name="keep-loaded-transaction-id" stories="load-transactions">
A loaded transaction keeps the `txn_id` from its file.
</requirement>

`inflow` is the sum of positive amounts. `outflow` is the sum of the absolute values of negative amounts, written as a positive number. `net` is `inflow - outflow`.
</requirement>

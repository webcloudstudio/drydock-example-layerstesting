## Planning Instructions

### Delivery Shape

A local Python command-line ledger persists accounts, categories, and transactions in SQLite through the shipped persistence library. Inputs are CLI arguments and CSV files; outputs are exact CSV reports or mutation results. The delivery flow is schema initialization, validated atomic imports, account and transaction operations, transfers, reports, focused tests, then full harness verification.

### Test and Acceptance Strategy

Each capability receives focused tests for valid output, invalid input, not-found behavior, atomicity, and balance rules. Reports are tested against computed fixtures rather than embedded report answers. `VERIFY-002` is the sole terminal full-suite story and runs the complete supplied harness with `Suite: full`; all other story tests remain scoped.

### Sequencing and Dependencies

Build the persistence boundary and schema before loaders and commands. Build parsers and validation before imports and mutations. Build mutation services before reports. Stage fixtures before verification. `VERIFY-001` follows the implemented capabilities; `VERIFY-002` depends on every implementation story.

### Source Conflicts and Gaps

No conflicting product definitions or blockers were found. The product is a CLI, not a web or API application; no authentication, deployment service, or external service behavior is required by the sources. Help text was absent from the source contract and is surfaced as a story criterion.
